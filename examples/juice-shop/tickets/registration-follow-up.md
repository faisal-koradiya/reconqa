# Story: Registration tidy-up

*Fictional follow-up ticket for the ReconQA demo. Some of its points were already answered by the
team (see qa/kb/registration/decisions.md); one is new.*

As a new customer, I want registration to be quick and clear so that I can start shopping.

## Acceptance criteria
1. After registering, the user is logged in automatically and sees the product list.
2. The user must accept the terms and conditions before the account is created.
3. If the email already has an account, the form shows "This email is already registered." under the email field.
4. The password advice checklist can be shown while typing a password.
5. The form says that the security question cannot be changed later.
