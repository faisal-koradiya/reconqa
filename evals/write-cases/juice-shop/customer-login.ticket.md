# Story: Returning customers log in

*Fictional fixture ticket (ReconQA eval). Juice Shop login screen.*

As a returning customer, I want to log in with my email and password so that I can see my basket and orders.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The login page has Email and Password fields; both are required.
2. The "Log in" button stays disabled until both fields are filled.
3. The password can be shown and hidden with an eye icon.
4. Wrong credentials show "Incorrect email or password. Please try again."
5. The error does not say which of the two fields was wrong.
6. A "Forgot your password?" link is shown under the password field.
7. Customers can also log in with Apple.
8. New visitors find a "Create account" link to the registration page.
