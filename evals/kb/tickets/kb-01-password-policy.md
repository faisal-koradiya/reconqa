# Story: Stronger passwords

*Fictional eval ticket (ReconQA knowledge-base eval).*

As a customer, I want clear password rules so that my account is safe.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A new password must be at least 8 characters long.
2. The character counter under the password field shows the real maximum of 40.
3. The user can switch on password advice to see a strength bar and a checklist.
